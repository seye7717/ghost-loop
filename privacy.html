Ghost Loop - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

The short version

Ghost Loop does not collect any data. At all.

Details

Ghost Loop is an offline arcade game. It does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Your high scores, unlocked content and settings are saved only on your own device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.
